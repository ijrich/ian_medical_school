## Consolidated feedback themes for the next rev

1. **Reframe the crystallizing moment.** The partner's illness (Jamie's spine tumor + multiple myeloma) should be the turning point for pursuing an MD, not bathing Walter. Walter is a vignette, not the pivot.

V2 still frames the decade-long detour as "needing to test" rather than as having pursued scaled impact after the first med school cycle didn't work out. Both drafts need a more decisive claim on why the physician role — not an adjacent one — is the right fit, both near and long-term.
