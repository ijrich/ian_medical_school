### Full email body

> Hello All!
>
> The applications for medical school (AMCAS for MD schools, AACOMAS for DO schools, and TMDSAS for Texas MD & DO schools) will open soon. As such, I have included some specific instructions for the application process below. Details on application component preparation the process itself are in the attached Application Handbook. Please read these instructions in their entirety- they cover many of the frequently asked questions during the application process. I will also post these (and subsequent) instructions on the Health Careers Advising Moodle. If you are no longer planning to apply to medical school this cycle (for entrance in Fall 2027), please let me know as soon as possible.
>
> Also, if you are graduating this semester, you will receive a separate email soon regarding post-Furman email addresses.
>
> 1. **Health Careers Advising Moodle:** All current Furman pre-health students have access to the Health Careers Advising Moodle. Application resources, such as presentations, workbooks, guidelines and instructions, archived information emails, and recommendation letter forms, will be posted to the moodle page.
>
>     Once you graduate, Furman will remove your Moodle access, so be sure to download all relevant materials before then. For those of you who have already graduated, we can send the relevant materials to you in a separate email. We are also exploring options in Box for alumni access, and will notify you if/when Box access becomes available.
>
> 2. **Cover Letter:** The Office of Pre-Professional Advising will be writing a cover letter for each of you to be included in your recommendation letter packet (see attached Application Handbook for more details on letters of recommendation). This takes significant time, as we want to be able to write the strongest possible letter for you. As such, send me an updated version of your CV/resume or activity list by May 15 so that we can begin preparing the letter. After you submit your application, also send a pdf of your completed application so that we can ensure our letter matches the information in your activity descriptions.
>
> 3. **AMCAS application (MD Schools):** The AMCAS application will open on May 5. The earliest submission date is May 28. The application will be available on the AMCAS website: https://www.aamc.org/students/applying/amcas/ (Be sure that you are completing the application for the 2027 entering class.) Plan to submit your application by the end of June at the latest. It can take up to 5 weeks to process your application. (If you are applying early decision, you should submit your application in enough time to be processed by the Aug 3 deadline.)
>
>     The instruction manual for the 2026-2027 AMCAS application can be found on the AMCAS website: https://students-residents.aamc.org/applying-medical-school/applying-medical-school-process/applying-medical-school-amcas/
>
>     The MSAR (Medical School Admissions Requirements) is an excellent resource for specific information about each medical school, such as average GPAs, MCATs, acceptances by state residency, etc. A one-year online subscription costs $28. More information can be found here: https://store.aamc.org/medical-school-admission-requirements-msar-for-u-s-and-canadian-medical-schools-online.html
>
> 4. **AACOMAS application (DO Schools):** The AACOMAS will open for submission on May 4. The application will be available on the AACOMAS website: https://aacomas.liaisoncas.org/
>
> 5. **TMDSAS application (Texas Schools- MD & DO):** The TMDSAS application will open on May 1. The earliest submission date is May 15.
>
> 6. **Transcripts:** Submit your transcript request form to academic records as soon as possible after the application opens and Furman grades are available for Spring 2026 (or Maymester 2026 if applicable).
>
> 7. **Application Release to Advisor:** For me to be able to upload letters of recommendation and assist you with the application process, you must release your application to the "advisor". Instructions for releasing the application can be found in the AMCAS, AACOMAS, and TMDSAS Instruction Manuals.
>
> 8. **Letters of Recommendation:** Detailed instructions on the letter of recommendation process are in the attached Application Handbook. Read these instructions carefully and thoroughly- nearly all of the questions we receive regarding letters of recommendation can be answered with information included in these instructions.
>
> 9. **Medical Schools:** You can add medical schools to your list after submitting the application. (You can NOT subtract schools after submission.) If you are still waiting on MCAT scores to decide how many schools to apply to, I recommend submitting the application with the medical schools you are sure you will be applying to regardless of MCAT score (Ex: the public schools in your state of residence), and then adding any "reach" schools after you receive your scores.
>
> Hope this helps, and have a great day!
>
> -Dr. B
