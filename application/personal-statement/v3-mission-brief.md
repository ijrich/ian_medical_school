## 4. Diagnosis of V2 — what works, what doesn't

### Works, keep

- The Walter scene is vivid and emotionally earned. It should remain in V3 — but as one illustrative vignette, not as the narrative pivot.
- The arc of "each step confirmed what I'd been told — but something was still missing" gives the essay forward motion.
- The surgeon moment in Jamie's arc ("not because he was kind, though he was, but because his depth of training…") names the right thing — depth of training unlocks trust. That line pattern should propagate.
- The FQHC line "I saw what a physician could be: someone whose years of preparation let them become a trusted resource" — the writer should use this voice for the physician-specific claim.

### Doesn't work, rebuild

- **Walter carries too much weight.** V2 stages the LNA work on the dementia/hospice unit as the crystallizing moment. Mark's critique (Round 2): it should be Jamie's illness, not bathing Walter.
- **"I designed a final test" frames clinical work as audition.** Mark's critique (Round 2): the FQHC shadowing and LNA work should read as *preparation* to be a physician, not *tests* of whether to become one. The "test" framing also invites the reader to ask why Ian needed twelve years to run an experiment he could have run in six months by shadowing.
- **The decade-of-detour is framed as "I took that seriously — I set out to test."** Mark's critique (Round 2): say Ian pivoted to scaled impact after the first med school cycle didn't work out, rather than claiming the decade was itself a planned test.
- **Why-MD-specifically is not decisive.** V2's "I want to bring everything I've learned and pair it with the clinical training to actually be in the room" doesn't close the door on nursing / PA / NP / patient advocate / social worker. Mark flagged this in both rounds. V3 must name something an MD does that adjacent roles can't.

## 5. The three reframes — the spine of V3

### 5.1 Jamie's illness is the crystallizing moment

Not Walter. Not the FQHC. Not the cumulative decade. Jamie's diagnosis and treatment is where Ian moves from "medicine is meaningful work I could support from the business side" to "I need to be the person in the room." The essay should sit inside this experience longer than V2 does — specific moments, specific physicians, specific decisions Ian watched get made.

Walter remains, but as a vignette that illustrates what Ian already knew by the time he met him. The LNA work is no longer where the decision gets made; it's where the decision gets lived out.

### 5.2 FQHC + LNA are preparation, not test

The FQHC shadowing with Dr. Emily Follo (~35 hrs, Sept–Dec 2025) and the LNA work on dementia/hospice are clinical experiences that equip Ian to be a compassionate and informed physician. V3 should frame them as apprenticeship and formation, not as auditions to see if Ian liked medicine enough.

### 5.3 Why an MD specifically

V3 has to land a decisive claim on why the physician role — not nurse, PA, NP, patient advocate, or social worker — is the right fit. Mark's framing from Round 1 points the direction: *physicians shape and drive the story*. V3 should name what the training gives Ian that other roles can't, ideally grounded in a moment Ian witnessed (Jamie's surgeon; Dr. Follo at the FQHC; someone from Ian's shadowing with Will Murphy at MSK) where the physician did something no one else in the room could.

## 6. Structural suggestions (non-binding)

The writer can structure V3 however serves the spine, but one path that fits Mark's Round 2 notes:

1. **Opening** — a scene from Jamie's arc (ideally the surgeon moment or another physician-at-the-decisive-edge moment). Opens *inside* the crystallizing experience, not outside it.
2. **The decade before** — condensed. Genetics → digital health → VC → impact investing, reframed: after the first cycle didn't work out, Ian went to work on scaled impact in healthcare, learned it at every layer. Not a test; a career.
3. **Jamie** — the longer treatment of the crystallizing moment. Multiple physician-moments, not just the surgeon.
4. **Preparation** — FQHC with Dr. Follo, the LNA work, Will Murphy at MSK. Framed as formation, not audition. Walter fits here as a vignette.
5. **The physician-specific claim** — decisive. What an MD does that adjacent roles can't. Earned by what precedes it.
6. **Close** — forward-looking, not retrospective.

## 7. Open questions the writer must get answered by Ian before drafting

These are the things V2 doesn't surface cleanly and that V3 needs. The writer should run this as an interview with Ian before writing the first draft.

**Jamie's arc (highest priority):**
1. Full timeline from diagnosis through current state. Where is she now? What's the prognosis?
2. Name at least three distinct physician-moments during her care — not just the spinal surgeon. Oncologist, radiation team, ICU, whoever was there. What did each one *do* that required their training?
3. When during her treatment did Ian know he needed to reapply? Before diagnosis, at diagnosis, during treatment, after?
4. One moment Ian still replays. What was it? Who was in the room, what did they say, what did Ian feel?

**Why MD specifically (second-highest priority):**
5. Why not NP/PA? Ian's done the LNA work — why doesn't a midlevel satisfy?
6. A single patient encounter (witnessed or lived) where a nurse or midlevel couldn't have navigated the situation, but a physician could. Concrete is the whole game here.

**The decade:**
7. Was the decade a planned "test" or a pivot after the first cycle didn't work? Ian should answer honestly — V3's framing of this paragraph depends on it.
8. Was there a specific moment at BVP or in impact-investing when Ian knew MD-less wasn't going to be enough? Or was it cumulative?

**FQHC with Dr. Follo:**
9. A specific encounter with Dr. Follo where Ian saw something that moved him. V2 says "trust and relationship" — too generic. What actually happened?
10. What does Ian mean when he says Dr. Follo helps patients "reshape their futures"? A scene, not an abstraction.

**LNA / hospice work:**
11. Beyond Walter — one other patient Ian wants in the essay, or does Walter carry the whole LNA moment?
12. What clinical knowledge did Ian find himself missing as an LNA that an MD would have given him?

**Will Murphy at MSK:**
13. Anything Ian saw shadowing Will at MSK that belongs in V3? (10 hrs 2025-12-12, 8 hrs 2026-04-06 per the service-hours log.) MSK is a credibility anchor if there's a usable scene.

**Reapplication framing:**
14. Does V3 mention reapplication explicitly, or let the "years ago I applied" line do the work? (V2 uses the opening line.) Whatever V3 chooses, it should be a deliberate choice.
